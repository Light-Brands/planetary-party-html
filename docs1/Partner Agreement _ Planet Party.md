## **Planetary Party 2025 – Organizational Partnership Agreement**

**Event Name:** Northeast Eco-Communities Convergence, Piloting “The Planetary Party”  
**Event Dates:** August 21–24, 2025  
**Location:** MOFGA Fairgrounds, Unity, ME (294 Crosby Brook Rd, Unity, ME 04988\)  
**Co-Produced By:** Permatours, Kinship Earth, and collaborating partners

**1\. Purpose of this Agreement**

This agreement outlines our shared intentions, commitments, and ways of working together to co-create the 2025 Planetary Party pilot event. It is rooted in relationship, mutual trust, and a shared vision of bioregional regeneration, community-led transformation, and joyful action.

**2\. Shared Values**

By entering into this agreement, all partners affirm alignment with the following guiding values:

* Collaboration over competition  
* Resource flows grounded in trust and place  
* Reciprocity, transparency, and care in all interactions  
* Inclusion of historically marginalized voices  
* Regenerative principles in action—ecologically, socially, economically

**3\. Partner Commitments**

Each partner organization agrees to:

* Actively contribute to the vision and success of the event through one or more of the following:  
  * Outreach and amplification  
  * Program design (workshops, performances, sessions)  
  * Logistical or material support  
  * Fundraising or financial contribution  
  * In-kind offerings (tools, supplies, infrastructure)  
  * Cultural, educational, or spiritual stewardship  
* Communicate clearly and respectfully with the core production team  
* Participate in at least one pre-event partner call or check-in  
* Represent the event accurately when promoting it publicly  
* Share any relevant updates, needs, or changes in a timely manner

**4\. What Partners Receive**

Partner organizations may receive (based on their level of contribution):

* Recognition on the event website, printed materials, and signage  
* Access to a private partner coordination channel on Discord  
* Discounted or comped event passes for team members  
* Opportunities to co-host or brand specific program tracks or spaces  
* Inclusion in post-event reports and storytelling  
* First consideration for partnership in future Planetary Party activations

**5\. Mutual Understanding**

This is a non-binding, trust-based agreement meant to establish clarity, not control. If at any point the partnership no longer feels aligned, either party may respectfully withdraw. We are committed to dialogue, repair, and adaptability as part of this co-creative process.

**6\. Communications & Weekly Partner Circle Check-ins**

* We’ll stay connected through a private **Discord channel** and regular **email updates**.  
* In addition, all partners are invited to attend a **30-minute weekly Partner Circle call**, designed to:  
  * Sync on key developments  
  * Share progress and emerging needs  
  * Surface ideas, opportunities, and challenges in real time  
  * Ensure our collaboration stays relational, transparent, and aligned  
* These calls are meant as light-touch sync points; each partner may have their own rhythm and schedule for more in-depth collaboration with the core organizing team.  
* Calls will be kept brief and intentional, with rotating facilitation and notes shared afterward for those who can’t attend.

**7\. Shared Storytelling & Documentation**

* With consent, we will highlight your organization’s role in our post-event storytelling.  
* You are welcome to share your own photos, videos, or takeaways—just tag @Permatours and @KinshipEarth when relevant, and uplift the broader movement.
