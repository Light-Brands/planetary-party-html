# **Planetary Party Governance Framework** 

V.0.1 \- Feb 2026 \- Fractal Mandate Model \- Living Document

# **1\. Purpose**

This governance framework exists to:

* Enable self-managed, high-trust collaboration  
* Increase clarity, speed, and accountability  
* Prevent bottlenecks and consensus paralysis  
* Maintain coherence with the Planetary Party’s purpose and principles

It is intentionally lightweight, adaptive, and will evolve as we grow.

# **2\. Core Architecture**

The Planetary Party operates as a **fractal ecosystem of working groups**, each with a defined mandate.

Each working group operates autonomously within its domain, aligned to shared strategic guardrails.

The Core Governance Circle stewards structural coherence across domains.

# **3.2.  Working Group Structure**

Each working group includes:

* 1 Accountable Steward  
* 1 Co-Steward (Energetic Mirror)  
* Supporters

### **3.2.1 Accountable Steward**

The Accountable Steward:

* Holds final decision authority within the group’s mandate  
* Sets deadlines  
* Calls meetings as needed  
* Synthesizes input  
* Moves work forward

It is accountability.

### **3.2.2 Co-Steward (Energetic Mirror)**

The Co-Steward:

* Provides reflection and balance  
* Supports coherence and integrity  
* Challenges blind spots  
* Does not override accountability

This creates shared leadership without shared paralysis.

### **3.2.3 Supporters**

Supporters:

* Contribute execution and perspective  
* Participate in co-sensing  
* Support delivery of group priorities

# **4\. Decision-Making Model**

## **4.1 Within a Working Group**

1. Co-sensing  
2. Integratation  
3. Movement

If alignment is not reached:

→ The Accountable Steward makes the final call.

Escalation to Core Governance only if:

* Cross-domain impact exists  
* Shared resources are involved  
* Brand-level implications arise  
* Structural governance questions emerge

Autonomy is default. Escalation is exception.

# **4.2. Core Governance Circle**

Core Governance:

* Reviews structural proposals  
* Stewards cross-domain coherence  
* Approves major shifts  
* Holds quarterly reflection on governance evolution

Governance meetings require:

* Written proposal  
* Pre-read  
* Clear decision point

No live drafting.

# **4.3. Strategy vs Execution Separation**

To prevent confusion:

### **Strategy & Coherence**

* Stewards Theory of Change  
* Holds narrative guardrails  
* Defines structural alignment

### **Execution Groups (Storytelling, Events, Fundraising, etc.)**

* Implement within strategic guardrails  
* Do not redefine core architecture

Strategy feeds execution. Execution does not rewrite strategy.

# **4.4. Escalation Protocol**

Escalation to Core Governance is appropriate when:

* Structural integrity is at stake  
* Resource allocation requires decision  
* Inter-group conflict cannot be resolved through mandate

Escalation is not default. Autonomy is default.

# **5\. Operational Discipline**

## **5.1 Single Source of Truth**

* Notion is the canonical operational system  
* No task lives only in chat  
* Documents must be linked in their appropriate working group

## **5.2 Task Visibility**

All tasks must include:

* Owner  
* Deadline  
* Working group tag  
* Status

Transparency reduces friction and emotional overload.

# **6\. Meeting Architecture**

### **Monday – Tactical Alignment**

Duration: 45 mins \- review Notion together

* 1 min check in per person / personal shares   
* Weekly priorities   
  * \- Upcoming meetings (who owns agenda and will lead, etc)   
* Clear ownership of tasks and assign in Notion   
* Identify blockers  
* Short and focused

### **Wednesday – Governance (As Needed)**

Duration: 1.5 hours

* Structural decisions  
* Cross-domain proposals  
* Pre-read required

### **Friday – Heart & Integration**

Duration: 1 hour

* Reflection  
* Relational coherence  
* Celebration

Emotional processing lives here, not in governance.

# **7\. Cultural Agreements**

To support coherence:

* We communicate directly and respectfully.  
* We default to trust.  
* We do not assume malintent.  
* We respond to tagged items within 48 hours.  
* We complete tasks we commit to or communicate early if blocked.  
* We separate emotional integration from structural decision-making.

We value velocity and relational integrity equally.

# **8\. Iteration**

This governance model is a living document.
