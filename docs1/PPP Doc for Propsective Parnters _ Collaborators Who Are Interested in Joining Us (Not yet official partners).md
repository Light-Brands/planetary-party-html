# **Welcome to the Planetary Party Protocol\!**

## **A shared ecosystem and protocol for bioregional coordination and systems redesign**

The **Planetary Party** is a living ecosystem that supports communities at a Bioregional level to come into shared intention, coordinate action, and evolve systems \- cultural, financial, governance, and relational \- in ways that mirror how healthy living systems function.

## **How Change Happens (Our Theory of Change)**

The *Planetary Party Protocol* (PPP) is built on a simple insight:

**The polycrisis is a [coordination failure](https://digitalrepository.unm.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1219&context=nrj). Coherence precedes effective action on any societal issue.**

When communities:

* listen deeply to place and people  
* map collectively  what’s happening together  
* Identify systemic solutions and flow resources  
* Cultivate trust and relationship  
* align around shared purpose  
* Co-design how decisions and resources flow

…then **collective action becomes wiser, faster, and more durable**.

PPP supports this through a 5-phase cycle:  
**Sense → See → Flow → Celebrate → Regenerate**  
This cycle creates the social, cultural, and relational conditions that enable regenerative systems to function with real impact.

## **Two Primary Ways to Enter the Field**

You’re invited to engage through **one (or both)** of the pathways outlined below.

## **A) Serve the Wider Ecosystem (Guilds & Partners)**

This pathway is for those who want to support the **Planetary Party ecosystem as a whole**, across multiple bioregions.

### **Clarifying roles**

* **Guild members** work within a defined functional guild  
* **Partners** are collaborators whose offerings support the PPP but **they are not participating in a specific guild**

Both are essential.

## **The Planetary Party (Initial) Guilds** 

* **Bioregional Intelligence** \- Maps people, projects, ecosystems, and resource flows across bioregions  
* **Event / Festival Production** \- Designs and stewards Planetary Party gatherings and activation arcs  
* **Governance** \- Develops consent-based decision-making, councils, and coordination frameworks  
* **Regenerative Capital Flows** \- Designs Flow Funding, participatory budgeting, and regenerative finance systems  
* **Capacity Building & Healing** \- Supports trust, leadership development, and relational resilience  
* **Storytelling** \- Shapes narrative through media, documentary, and storytelling frameworks  
* **Guild Ecosystem Weaving** \- Holds onboarding, communications, and cross-guild coherence  
* **Technology** \- Builds platforms, dashboards, and coordination / co-creation tools  
* **Living Systems & Solutions** \- Surfaces and supports real-world regenerative practices

## **B) Participate in or Activate a Specific Bioregion**

This pathway is for **place-based participation**.

Current bioregions we’re actively engaging include, but are not limited to:

* **Colombia** (Aguaclara, Sinú, Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Bogotá, Cali)  
* **Jamaica** (Rastafari Indigenous Village, Montego Bay)  
* **Mexico** (Cloud Forest of Veracruz & Water Forest \- Valley of Mexico)  
* **Guatemala** (Indigenous-led work around Lake Atitlán)  
* **Northeast of Turtle Island (U.S.)** (bioregional activation launching 2026\)

## **What It Means to Co-Create**

Co-creating with the Planetary Party Protocol means:

* Clear roles and expectations  
* Respect for intellectual property and authorship  
* Transparency around reciprocity and stewardship  
* Trust-based collaboration over bureaucracy

When alignment is mutual, **formal collaboration is confirmed through a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)** that clarifies:

* scope of collaboration  
* roles and contributions  
* duration and boundaries  
* IP and reciprocity (if applicable)
